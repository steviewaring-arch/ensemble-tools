The systemLive. Switch app and state, or just use it – open sections, Export, Presets, Show, Preview (F).

Three apps, one frame. Each keeps what makes it itself: Tempo’s screen-sized preview, Rubato’s transport, Tutti’s recording.

The islandOne object, four states. Same height everywhere: 44 px, controls 36 px inside.

The island only ever holds what you do to the view and what you take away. What’s in it depends on the app and the moment: Tempo’s Show, Rubato’s transport and Randomise, Tutti’s play controls only when the source moves. When room runs short, labels become icons before anything is dropped, and Presets shrinks to its tile.

Sheets grow out of whatever opened them – Export from the island, Presets from its capsule, Randomise’s options from the island in Rubato – and one at a time. Esc or a click on the work closes them.

The rulesFor these three and whatever comes next

  • The work fits, then fillsBy default the work fits the clear space beside the panel and above the island, on the app’s own colour. Preview fills the window with it, and the controls stay on top.
  • Four things floatPanel (left, making), island (bottom centre, showing and taking away), Presets (bottom right, choosing), chips with Preview and light/dark (top right). 12 px from the edges, bottoms aligned, frosted at 88%.
  • Sheets grow from their sourceOne at a time. Esc or a click on the work closes them.
  • Preview and light/dark, top rightSame place in every app, beside the chips. F for Preview.
  • Status lives in the islandProgress, recording, saved. No other toasts.
  • Only what appliesNo presets, no capsule. No video, no transport. Controls that depend on something switched off disappear, and the space closes up.
  • Three levels of depthSection card, group, detail. Shut sections say what’s set.
  • One of each controlOne slider, segmented control, menu, toggle, list row, button set. Four short options in a row, five to eight in a grid, more or longer ones in a menu.
  • Help on everythingEvery control has a tooltip, on hover or keyboard focus. Hints are one line; longer help is a detail.
  • Labels are the contractThe tests find controls by their names, so names move with their controls, word for word.

Order of workOne app at a time, on a ui branch, tested each time

  1. System files and the component page

    Tokens v2, components v2 (frame, island, sheets, sections, controls), the layout parts of core.js (card() gains a summary; new island() and sheet()), shared/UI.md. The component page goes up at /ui/ with every part in every state, light and dark, for your sign-off.

  2. Tempo

    Moved, then tempo_check, split_check and parity. The panel results there change on purpose and are listed as such. Before and after screenshots at 1440 × 900 and 1280 × 800, light and dark. Merge.

  3. Rubato

    Same again, with rubato_regress and rubato_features. Exports must stay byte-identical.

  4. Tutti

    Onto the shared files with its exports checked against 6.0, then the frame. Then the Squarespace embed at its real size.
